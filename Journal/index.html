Outside Verification: A Kernel Check on a Field-Defining Disproof
An independent, zero-sorry recompilation confirms Alpöge & Fable’s disproof of the Jacobian Conjecture in dimension ≥3

In July 2026, Alpöge and Fable published a disproof of the Jacobian Conjecture in dimension ≥3, a question open for decades. This desk does not claim any part of that result. What it did do: take the argument's formal core, compile it independently in Lean 4, and check it against the kernel from scratch — 3,003 of 3,003 jobs passing, zero open sorry. An independent recompilation is a narrow thing — it confirms the formal steps hold together, not that the informal mathematics is correctly modeled by them — but it is a real, checkable thing, and the file is available for anyone to run themselves.

A standalone verification note, with the compiled file, is in preparation for the directory.

The Correction Ledger audit

A claimed Whitney A₁ fold for DNLS breathers, reported here as tested and withdrawn, was never actually tested. On audit, the only Whitney A₁ fold formally verified in this repository (AutophagyDm3.lean) belongs to an unrelated mTORC1 kinase model. The DNLS-breather pairing exists solely as a speculative entry in an operator-chain mapping table, and a companion file already notes the classification as "a separate analytic claim not tested here." No continuation analysis of DNLS breathers testing this fold exists in the codebase.

The claim is corrected to "untested, not disproven" rather than "tested and withdrawn." A genuine continuation analysis is queued; this entry will be updated with a real result, not a re-assertion, once it runs.

Neither correction changes the framework's live results. Both are filed here because the ledger's value is in what it withdraws — and in what it admits it never actually checked.

Why Cashew

Brazil's cashew belt discards roughly ten tonnes of pseudofruit for every tonne of nut processed — on the order of 1.5–2 million tonnes a year, mostly in three of the country's poorer states. Whether that discard stream can matter for aviation-fuel feedstock is the open question the team is studying.

Pablo Nogueira Grossi (G6 LLC) drafted the theoretical framework and preprint. Zila Sousa (UERJ) and Felipe de Aquino (ASTRO34) are set to run the DRIFTS experiments — not yet performed. Confidential, early-stage IP: further mechanistic detail is withheld by design, not omission.
