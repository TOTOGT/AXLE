## Main post

**New issue out — *Imaginary Origin*, The Generative Science Journal, Vol. Ω No. 13: "Ten Chapters on a Fifth Dimension."**

The front page is about adding a fifth dimension to physics. Kaluza did it in 1921 and
Klein explained in 1926 why we do not see it. The page also works one calculation by
hand: in five dimensions, a contact structure exists exactly where F∧F ≠ 0, which is
where the electric and magnetic fields are not perpendicular.

The page I would point students to is page 4, the corrections. Several of our own
Lean theorems compiled but did not prove what their names said. One reads, in effect,
"if the return state differs from the base point, then it differs from the base point."
We print what was wrong, where it is marked, and what the repair does not show: it
does not show the claims false, only that the Lean did not prove them.

Page 5 turns from mathematics to money. It is a working analysis, from public
documents, of how a town near the city might give visitors a reason to stay for
dinner: the places people can visit now, and a loop of eat, shop, stay, explore and
come back. It says what it checked, what is second-hand, and what it could not find.

The same issue carries the September Population Employment Gap update (40.79%, down
from 40.91%) in a short note, and Part 8 of the Reading Room serial on page 6.

Read it: https://totogt.github.io/AXLE/Journal/vol13.html
