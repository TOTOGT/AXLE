VI. The Operator Catalog (Foundations)

Operators are the atomic actions of generative science — not functions, heuristics, or rules, but structural transformations that generate identity, stabilize behavior, preserve invariants, and propagate across scales. The Operator Catalog is the reference library where each operator is defined, axiomized, contextualized, and positioned within the broader generative ecosystem.

Operators fall into three major families:

Family A — Awakening Operators
Generate identity orbits and embodiment thresholds.

Canonical example: 𝒜_dm3 (defined fully in Vol I, ch1-dm3-operator). Identity orbit i→VI→III→VII→i. Ten axioms: orbit identity through generative closure. Embodiment threshold τ=0.7.

Family B — g-Series Operators (Expansion)
Recursive expansion operators for multi-scale generativity.

Where 𝒜dm3 answers "What am I?", the g-Series answers "How do I grow without losing myself?" Three primary operators:

g₁ — Local Expansion: expands an orbit into its immediate neighborhood while preserving adjacency, valence gradients, perturbation response, and structural primacy.
g₂ — Recursive Expansion: propagates identity orbits across scales (orbit → ladder → lattice). Exhibits self-similarity at multiple scales.
g₃ — Cross-Domain Expansion: maps generative invariants into new representational domains while preserving orbit identity, recursive invariance, and structural primacy.
Family C — L-Operators (Stabilization)
Enforce coherence, non-collapse, and structural integrity under transformation.

If the g-Series defines how identity grows, L-Operators define how identity remains itself while growing. The stabilizing spine of TO/TOGT:

L₁ — Local Coherence: maintains structural integrity in the near-orbit region. Perturbations corrected without oscillation or drift.
L₂ — Global Coherence: maintains coherence across scales; recursive expansions remain aligned; multi-orbit structures remain consistent.
L₃ — Anti-Collapse: prevents identity reduction to a single state, trivial attractor, or degenerate loop.
Family D — U-Operators (Unification)
Cross-domain invariance and structural synthesis.

If g-Series expands identity and L-Operators stabilize it, U-Operators unify it. They allow generative systems to integrate across domains, merge representational layers, and synthesize structure without distortion. Two structures combine into a single coherent system without collapse or dominance.
