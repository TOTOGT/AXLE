Proof Library Advances, With Its Gaps Left in Plain View
Lean 4 formalization of the Principia Orthogona series proceeds; unproved steps are marked, not hidden

The series is being written into the Lean 4 proof assistant with the Mathlib library. Some results are proved and machine-checkable; many steps still carry an open sorry, and several hypotheses are stated as explicit axioms rather than derived. Across 121 of the authors’ own files the library is unfinished — and the pages say so. The full directory and the theorem registry remain open for inspection.
