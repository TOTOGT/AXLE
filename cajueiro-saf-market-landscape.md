## Market Context: The Mandate Stack

Three overlapping mandates create a structurally captive demand for SAF that no airline or aerospace manufacturer can ignore:

| Mandate | Geography | Requirement | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ICAO CORSIA Phase 1** | All international flights | SAF or carbon offset for emissions above 2019 baseline | January 2027 (hard) |
| **ReFuelEU Aviation** | EU-departing flights | 2% SAF blend by 2025 · 6% by 2030 · 70% by 2050 | Live now |
| **US IRA §45Z** | USA | $1.25–1.75/gallon SAF tax credit | Through 2027 (expiring) |
| **UK SAF Mandate** | UK-departing flights | 2% SAF by 2025 · 10% by 2030 | Live now |
| **Brazil RenovaBio** | Brazil | Fuel decarbonization credit (CBIO) system | Live |
| **ANAC SAF Framework** | Brazil | National SAF procurement under development | 2026–2027 |

**The technology gap:** Global SAF production (≈ 1 billion litres in 2024) covers < 0.5% of aviation demand. HEFA (hydroprocessed esters) dominates today; ATJ (alcohol-to-jet) is the scalable pathway — and catalyst selectivity is the rate-limiting constraint. This is where B-MCM-22 + O-index enters.

## Section V · IP Competitive Landscape — Catalyst Technology

The B-MCM-22 + O-index process method competes with and complements existing ATJ catalyst IP:

| Technology / Assignee | Pathway | Weakness vs B-MCM-22 + O-index |
|---|---|---|
| **Honeywell UOP — Ecofining™** | HEFA; ATJ-SPK license | HEFA has limited feedstock flexibility; ATJ version uses ZSM-5 (higher coke, lower selectivity at cashew-ethanol purity) |
| **Axens / IFP — Futurol™** | Cellulosic ethanol + ATJ | Full-chain license; does not address operator-order selectivity at the zeolite step |
| **ASTM D4806 / ZSM-5 standard** | Industry default ATJ zeolite | ZSM-5 has lower aromatic selectivity; more coke; B-MCM-22 advantage is the differentiator |
| **ExxonMobil — MTG process** | Methanol-to-gasoline (zeolite based) | Different feedstock; ZSM-5 based; our O-index is a superior selectivity method applicable across the family |
| **LanzaTech / LanzaJet** | Gas fermentation → ethanol → ATJ | No proprietary zeolite IP; licensing candidate not competitor |
| **Gevo — isobutanol ATJ** | Different alcohol substrate | Complementary; their zeolite finishing step is improvable by B-MCM-22 |
