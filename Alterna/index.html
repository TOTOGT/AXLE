Alternating Forms Vanish Beyond Dimension:
A Mechanized Proof in Lean 4 with Application to Contact Integrability

Pablo Nogueira Grossi  ·  G6 LLC, Newark NJ  ·  ORCID 0009-0000-6496-2186
June 2026  ·  doi:10.5281/zenodo.20710023  ·  github.com/TOTOGT/AXLE
MSC 2020: 15A75, 53D10, 68V20  ·  Keywords: alternating forms · Lean 4 · Mathlib · Nijenhuis tensor · contact geometry · dm³
Abstract. Any alternating m-linear map on a rank-n module over a commutative ring is identically zero whenever m > n. We give a machine-checked proof of this fact in Lean 4 using two Mathlib lemmas: AlternatingMap.map_linearDependent and LinearIndependent.fintype_card_le_finrank. The proof is twelve lines. As a first application, a pointwise dimension count shows that the Nijenhuis tensor NJ vanishes on the Reeb-orbit direction Γ of the dm³ contact 3-manifold introduced in [Gro26]. The paper locates this result in a three-level integrability tower and identifies precisely what the deeper levels require.

1 · Introduction

The Nijenhuis tensor is the fundamental obstruction to the integrability of an almost complex structure. The Newlander–Nirenberg theorem [NN57] gives a sufficient condition—vanishing of NJ—for a 2n-dimensional almost complex manifold (n ≥ 2) to carry a holomorphic atlas. Its proof uses elliptic PDE theory and is non-trivial even in the smooth category.

The dm³ framework [Gro26] places an almost complex structure J on a contact 3-manifold (M, ξ) with contact form α and Reeb field R. The tangent bundle decomposes as TM = ξ ⊕ ⟨R⟩, where ξ = ker α has rank 2 and the Reeb direction Γ = ⟨R⟩ has rank 1. A natural question is when NJ|P = 0 can be proved by purely algebraic means, avoiding analysis entirely.

Main result

Theorem 1 (NJ|Γ = 0, algebraic). Let Γ = ⟨R⟩ be the rank-1 Reeb-orbit fibre of the dm³ contact 3-manifold. Any alternating bilinear map F : Γ × Γ → V is identically zero. In particular, NJ(X, Y) = 0 for all X, Y ∈ Γ.

The proof is a pointwise dimension count (m = 2 > 1 = rank Γ) formalised in Lean 4 and verified by Mathlib. Zero sorrys.

2 · The Algebraic Vanishing Theorem

Theorem 2 — Alternating Vanishing (general)

Let R be a commutative ring, V a finitely generated R-module of rank n, and W any R-module. If m > n then every alternating m-linear map f : Vm → W is the zero map.

Proof sketch. Any m-tuple from a rank-n module with m > n is linearly dependent [Mathlib, LinearIndependent.fintype_card_le_finrank]. Alternating maps vanish on linearly dependent inputs [Mathlib, AlternatingMap.map_linearDependent]. □

The complete Lean 4 proof (Mathlib 4, commit checked 2026-05-10):

theorem alternating_vanishes_beyond_dim
    {R : Type*} [CommRing R]
    {V : Type*} [AddCommGroup V] [Module R V] [Module.Finite R V]
    {W : Type*} [AddCommGroup W] [Module R W]
    {m : ℕ} (f : AlternatingMap R V W (Fin m))
    (hm : Module.finrank R V < m) : f = 0 := by
  ext v
  apply f.map_linearDependent   -- [Mathlib, AlternatingMap.map_linearDependent]
  intro hind
  have hcard := hind.fintype_card_le_finrank  -- [Mathlib, LinearIndependent]
  simp at hcard; omega

Non-example. The hypothesis m > n is essential. The symplectic form Ω on ℝ² is an alternating bilinear form (m = 2 = n) that is non-degenerate. Theorem 2 says nothing in that regime; Newlander–Nirenberg is the tool there.

The Newlander–Nirenberg theorem [NN57] is not needed at any level. Level 1 needs none of it. Levels 2d and 2d+t use the 2-dimensional case of integrability—which predates [NN57] and requires only 2D elliptic theory—and the contact identity ιR dα = 0 respectively.

4 · What Is and Is Not Novel

Not novel

  • The algebraic fact (Theorem 2) is classical — see Lang [Lan02, Ch. 19], Bourbaki Algebra III, for finite-dimensional vector spaces over a field.

Novel — 3 contributions

  • (i) Lean 4 proof over arbitrary commutative rings + finitely generated modules (not just fields + finite-dim vector spaces). No prior machine-checked proof at this generality existed.
  • (ii) Identification of this algebraic mechanism as the exact tool for Level-1 vanishing in the dm³ tower.
  • (iii) Correct account of Levels 2/3: isothermal coordinates (analytic), not the false "d²α=0 suffices" claim.
