Hour House / Principia Orthogona ESL lesson programme. Lessons are organized into three tiers:

- `esl-101/` — Foundations (A2–B2): Lesson 03 (Number 33), Lesson 03b (Chladni Plates), Lesson 04 (USA Top 10), Lesson 05 (Your First Reel)
- `esl-102/` — Intermediate (B1–C1): Lesson 02 (Cajueiro Principle), Lesson 02b (Galilean Confluence), Crop Circles 1 & 2, Lesson 05b (Media Kit), Lesson 06 (WorldQuant Top 10), Lesson 07 (Your First Alpha), **Lesson 08 (Saying How You Know)**
- `esl-103/` — Advanced (B2–D2): Lesson 01 (Vitruvian Approximation), Voynich Manuscript (final project), **Lesson 09 (The Parameter That Disappeared)**
- `104` — English for Researchers, a 16-week capstone track (`course-16weeks.html`, this folder)
- `101/`, `102/`, `103/` — the **dm³ track**, sixteen weeks each (`dm3-101-w01.html` … ), not the ESL tiers. The ESL lessons live in the `esl-` folders; `index.html` links to those.

**Lessons 08 and 09 are a pair.** 08 asks whether the evidence supports the claim at all — *warrant*, five tags, B1–B2 with a C1 extension. 09 asks how many cases the claim covers — *scope*, C1–D2, and opens on the analemma and the lemniscate. Read 08 first.

**After school, outside the ESL tiers:** `zero-sorries.html` — an eight-session proof course for grades 5–8, built inside New York City's September 2026 restriction on student-facing generative AI. A proof checker generates nothing, so nothing in it is a chatbot. Companion file `counting.lean`.

`index.html`, `caela-framework.html`, and `g6-earth-house.html` stay at the AULA root. `assets/` holds loose, unreferenced files (screenshots, a zip) kept for the record.
