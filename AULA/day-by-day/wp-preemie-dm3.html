Abstract. A preterm birth forces the developing organism across multiple biological threshold events before the attractor basins supporting those transitions are fully formed. This paper maps four such thresholds — pulmonary surfactant synthesis, cardiac conduction, neural attractor formation, and immune proofreading — onto the dm³ operator chain G = U∘F∘K∘C, using the kernel-verified autophagy result (AXLE/AutophagyDm3.lean) as the topological anchor. The paper identifies one new Lean 4 obligation (surfactant network CRNT deficiency), states all claims with their evidence class, and cross-references the relevant chapters of Principia Orthogona Vol VI. The clinical correlate is the Preemie Manual's growth tracker, where WHO percentile suppression before term-equivalent age is the practical instance of the same principle.

2. Domain 1 — Pulmonary Surfactant Synthesis

The biological threshold

Pulmonary surfactant — a lipoprotein complex secreted by type II alveolar pneumocytes — reduces alveolar surface tension and prevents collapse on expiration. Its synthesis depends on the transcription factor TTF-1 (NKX2-1), which drives expression of surfactant proteins SP-B and SP-C. documented

SP-B synthesis is functionally absent before approximately 24–26 weeks gestation and reaches adequate levels around 34–36 weeks. The transition is not gradual: below a gestational threshold, respiratory distress syndrome (RDS) is near-universal in the absence of exogenous surfactant; above it, RDS incidence drops sharply. documented (Jobe & Ikegami 1987; Whitsett & Weaver 2015, New England Journal of Medicine)

dm³ mapping

In dm³ terms: the TTF-1/SP-B/SP-C synthesis cascade is a candidate for the same fold structure as the autophagy switch. The C operator is the hormonal and mechanical compression of fetal breathing movements and glucocorticoid signaling; K is the TTF-1 activation threshold; F is the fold at which lamellar body formation commits; U is sustained surfactant secretion. model

Lean obligation [LEAN-NEEDED-1]: Does the TTF-1/NKX2-1 → SP-B/SP-C transcription network have CRNT deficiency δ = 2? If yes, the contact morphism to Xauto is supportable on topological grounds. If no, the fold claim must be downgraded to phenomenological analogy only.

The network to check: TTF-1 autoregulation loop + SP-B/SP-C feedback on lamellar body maturation. Relevant literature for network topology: Whitsett et al. 2010 (Physiological Reviews), Xu et al. 2012 (Journal of Biological Chemistry).

Clinical correlate

Antenatal corticosteroids (betamethasone/dexamethasone) accelerate surfactant maturation by acting upstream of the TTF-1 threshold — a clinical intervention that effectively shifts the fold threshold earlier. documented (Roberts & Dalziel 2006, Cochrane Database). This is consistent with the dm³ reading: the C operator (compression) is being augmented pharmacologically.

3. Domain 2 — Cardiac Conduction

Connection to book6 Ch 15

Chapter 15 of Vol VI (Cardiac Rhythms — Wenckebach, Lyapunov, and T* = 2π) establishes that cardiac conduction ratios (3:2 Wenckebach) map onto the dm³ operator chain, with the honest caveat that only the 3:2 ratio is a genuine Fibonacci convergent.

Prematurity-specific disruption

Preterm infants show high rates of cardiac arrhythmia, including bradycardia of prematurity — the most common arrhythmia in this population. The underlying mechanism is incomplete maturation of the autonomic nervous system's cardiac control, specifically the parasympathetic (vagal) tone that stabilizes sinus rhythm. documented (Poets et al. 1994, Pediatrics; Fairchild et al. 2016, Frontiers in Physiology)

In dm³ terms: the Lyapunov attractor basin for stable sinus rhythm (the limit cycle Γ of Ch 15) has not fully formed in the preterm autonomic system. The fold at K* — the threshold between stable rhythm and bradycardic episode — is crossed repeatedly because the attractor basin is shallow. model

This is falsifiable in principle: HRV (heart rate variability) data from preterm infants should show lower Lyapunov stability than term infants of matched corrected age, with convergence toward term values as corrected age increases. Such data exist in the literature (Lake et al. 2002, American Journal of Physiology) but have not been fitted to the dm³ parameters. open

4. Domain 3 — Neural Attractor Formation

Connection to book6 Ch 16 and Ch 17

Chapter 16 (Hopfield Networks) establishes the energy function E = −½ΣWᵢⱼSᵢSⱼ as a genuine Lyapunov function (Hopfield 1982, kernel-verified). Chapter 17 covers gamma oscillations and Communication-Through-Coherence (Fries 2015, cited as an active hypothesis, not settled fact).

Prematurity-specific disruption

Myelination of cortical and subcortical tracts proceeds through the third trimester and into the first postnatal years, but the most rapid phase occurs between 28 and 40 weeks gestation. In the preterm brain, this window occurs ex utero, in the NICU environment rather than in utero. documented (Counsell et al. 2006, Pediatrics; Ball et al. 2013, Brain)

Incomplete myelination reduces conduction velocity and synchrony between neural populations. In Hopfield network terms: the weight matrix W is sparser and less structured than in a term brain at equivalent developmental stage. The energy wells (attractor basins) are shallower, and the network capacity (~0.138N, Ch 16) is lower. model

The practical consequence: preterm infants at corrected term age show different EEG coherence patterns than term newborns, consistent with shallower attractor basins. documented (Stjerna et al. 2015, NeuroImage). Whether the dm³ parameters (ε₀, μ_max) can be fitted to this data is an open calibration problem. open

The music and voice finding

Auditory stimulation in the NICU — including the mother's recorded voice and selected music — has documented effects on preterm neurodevelopment. Randomized controlled trials show improvements in feeding behavior, physiological stability, and neurodevelopmental outcomes at follow-up. documented (Loewy et al. 2013, Pediatrics; Filippa et al. 2019, Acta Paediatrica)

In attractor terms: the mother's voice is a known input to an auditory cortex that is forming its weight matrix in real time. Consistent, meaningful auditory input during this window may contribute to attractor depth in the relevant neural circuits. This is a plausible mechanistic reading, not a verified one. open

5. Domain 4 — Immune Proofreading

Connection to book6 Ch 12, 13, 14

Chapters 12–14 cover VDJ recombination, thymic selection (three-stage proofreading cascade, ~2% of thymocytes mature), and germinal center affinity maturation. These are the best-documented instances of the C∘K∘F∘U operator chain in immunology.

Prematurity-specific disruption

Thymic development is substantially complete by 28 weeks gestation, but the thymus continues to mature through term. Preterm infants have reduced naive T cell output, lower thymic index, and altered regulatory T cell proportions compared to term infants. documented (Gibbons et al. 2014, Journal of Allergy and Clinical Immunology; Rechavi et al. 2015, Science Translational Medicine)

In dm³ terms: the three-stage proofreading cascade of Ch 13 (positive selection at K*, negative selection window closing at K**) is compressed in the preterm thymus. The ~2% mature T cell output rate — which depends on the precision of two sequential threshold crossings — may be altered when the attractor basins for each stage are incompletely formed. model

Lean obligation [LEAN-NEEDED-2]: The thymic selection cascade in Ch 13 is framed as a φ→η→Δ naming analogy, explicitly not a numerical fit. For the preterm mapping to be more than phenomenological, the two-threshold structure (K*, K**) needs a CRNT or equivalent topological characterization comparable to [LEAN-NEEDED-1]. This is a harder problem than the surfactant network because the relevant species (TCR signal strength, MHC affinity) are continuous rather than discrete. open

7. The Clinical Instrument

The Preemie Manual growth tracker implements the most immediate practical consequence of this framework: WHO Child Growth Standards are suppressed before corrected age zero and the Fenton 2013 preterm chart is cited instead. This is not merely a technical fix — it is the same principle as the fold argument: a reference standard built for term-equivalent biology does not apply before the relevant thresholds have been crossed.

The tracker is valid for any child, preterm or full-term, from term-equivalent age to 5 years (WHO standards), and for preterm infants from birth using the Fenton chart (22–50 weeks postmenstrual age).

8. Open Obligations

[LEAN-NEEDED-1] — Surfactant network CRNT deficiency.
Check TTF-1/NKX2-1 → SP-B/SP-C network for δ = 2 using the same method as AutophagyDm3.lean. If confirmed, surfactant synthesis can be stated as a contact morphism of the autophagy fold. If not confirmed, §2 of this paper is downgraded to phenomenological.
[LEAN-NEEDED-2] — Thymic selection CRNT or equivalent.
The two-threshold structure (K*, K**) of thymic selection (Ch 13) needs topological characterization. The continuous nature of TCR signal strength makes this harder than the discrete molecular networks already checked. A viable approach may be a reduced ODE model of positive/negative selection dynamics, analogous to WP-31C's treatment of autophagy, and then checking deficiency on the resulting network graph.
[OPEN-1] — HRV Lyapunov fitting.
Fit dm³ Lyapunov parameters (μ_max, basin radius) to published HRV data from preterm infants (Lake et al. 2002 or equivalent) at matched corrected ages. This requires the calibration pipeline of WP-31 — operationalize, estimate, validate, iterate — applied to cardiac data. The rate constant problem that defeated WP-30 on autophagy is likely easier here because cardiac timescales are directly measurable.
[OPEN-2] — Neural attractor calibration.
Map EEG coherence data (Stjerna et al. 2015 or equivalent) to Hopfield network parameters. Specifically: does the EEG coherence deficit in preterm infants at corrected term age correspond to a measurable reduction in the effective connection weight W, or to increased noise in the energy landscape? These make different predictions about recovery trajectory.
