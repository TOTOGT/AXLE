AI Formation for the Faithful

A free, tiered curriculum offered in the spirit of Magnifica Humanitas · G6 LLC · Pablo Nogueira Grossi · Newark, NJ
Draft · for the Archdiocese of Newark

1 · The Holy Father's call

In Magnifica Humanitas — "On Safeguarding the Human Person in the Time of Artificial Intelligence," signed 15 May 2026 on the 135th anniversary of Leo XIII's Rerum Novarum — Pope Leo XIV names artificial intelligence the moral question of the age, and sets its measure against the dignity of the human person:

"To disarm does not mean rejecting technology, but preventing it from dominating humanity." — Pope Leo XIV, Magnifica Humanitas (2026)

The antidote to domination is understanding. A tool dominates the ignorant and serves the formed. To keep the human person at the center of this age — never, in the Holy Father's words, "replaced or surpassed" — the faithful must be helped to comprehend these tools rather than fear them or be ruled by them. That is formation, and formation is the Church's own work of the new evangelization.

2 · What is offered — one ladder, first steps to the frontier

A complete, free body of teaching materials, already written and in use, structured so a person can enter with no background and climb as far as they wish. It joins an existing vocational program (the Aula) to an advanced open corpus (Principia Orthogona) — one continuous path.

First steps · the Aula Gentle, plain-language digital and AI literacy, taught beside practical vocational courses already running — English, IT for the court system, and a growing set. The on-ramp: what these tools are, what they are not, and how to use them without being used.
The ascent · Principia Orthogona For those who continue: rigorous, machine-checked mathematics and the frontier of AI-assisted reasoning (formalization in Lean, and beyond). The same material a graduate student would meet — made reachable by the steps below it.
Terms All materials free and openly licensed (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0). No cost to the Archdiocese. Deliverable online, in a parish hall, or in a classroom. Beginner or advanced tracks drawn from the same source.

3 · Grounded in Newark — The Ballantine Estate Proposal

To anchor this work locally, G6 LLC is pursuing the acquisition of the historic Ballantine Estate at 229 Ballantine Parkway in Forest Hill. This 15,000-square-foot property features a historical precedent of institutional and educational use, having previously operated as a private school campus. Its distinct, dual-structure layout mirrors the tiered nature of the curriculum itself:

  • The Main Mansion (The Aula Classroom): The grand common rooms and 15 bedrooms provide an ideal setting for plain-language community digital literacy courses, vocational training seminars, and housing for visiting scholars.
  • The Carriage House (The Advanced R&D Lab): The detached multi-level structure will be converted into a dedicated technical laboratory for machine-checked logic, open-source tool construction, and advanced AI-assisted reasoning.

By restoring this post-Prohibition landmark back to its historical identity as a space for learning, the project provides the Archdiocese with an immediate, physical center of excellence for modern parish formation without demanding structural construction funds from pastoral budgets.

4 · An open door — why the invitation is now

Artificial intelligence is still young enough that the distance from beginner to practitioner is short. A person with sound formation and a little courage can already do — for a parish, a family, a trade — what until recently asked for a specialist. These openings are real, and they are widest now, while the ground is still level; as the tools mature, the easy footholds close. So the call is not only to learn but to take part: to gather the fruit within reach and put it to honest use, in keeping with the dignity of work the Church has upheld since Rerum Novarum. Come in early — and bring others in with you.

5 · A word on why this is offered

The human person at the center is not, to this author, an abstraction. It is a conviction paid for personally — the reason these materials are given rather than sold. That testimony can be shared privately with the appropriate office if it would help.

6 · The ask

A brief meeting with the office responsible for catechesis, education, or evangelization in the Archdiocese, to review the materials, evaluate the 229 Ballantine spatial plan, and consider a no-cost pilot — a single parish or school, beginner track — with a path to the advanced corpus for those who continue. If it bears fruit locally, it is ready to be offered more widely, and, in time, brought to the attention of the relevant Vatican dicastery.
