Predictions and Solutions for Aminoglycoside Resistance Riboswitches via Contact-Geometric Operator Analysis. Aminoglycoside-sensing riboswitches in the 5′ leader of aac/aad resistance genes undergo an inducible conformational switch — OFF (SD2 sequestered) to ON (SD2 exposed) — that activates resistance enzyme expression only in the presence of drug.

↗ Zenodo · D1 · 10.5281/zenodo.20567318 Published · June 2026
Abstract

No crystal structure of the full ON state exists; the switching kinetics have not been measured directly; and the universality of the switch across integron contexts is not established.

We apply the TOGT/GTCT contact-geometric operator framework to map the riboswitch conformational cycle to the operator chain G = U ∘ F ∘ K ∘ C on a dm³ contact manifold. The fold operator F (Whitney A₁ singularity) maps to the stem competition bifurcation; the curvature threshold κ* maps to the free-energy barrier between OFF and ON stems.

F ↔ stem competition bifurcation
κ*_ribo = free-energy barrier between OFF stem and ON stem · Whitney A₁ singularity

Keywords: riboswitch, aminoglycoside, antibiotic resistance, contact geometry, κ*, OFF-lock antagonist, SHAPE probing, TOGT

Three Falsifiable Predictions
P1 · SHAPE / DMS Probing

The switching free-energy barrier is linear in κ*_ribo, measurable by SHAPE/DMS probing. The dm³ Lipschitz bound gives the proportionality constant without free parameters. Falsified if: the barrier vs κ* plot is non-linear (R² < 0.9 by linear regression).

P2 · OFF-Lock Antagonist Geometry

An effective antagonist raises κ* at the fold point — steric bulk at the switching junction suffices, full pocket occupancy is not necessary. Molecules that increase κ*_ribo above threshold will lock the OFF state regardless of binding affinity to the aptamer core. Falsified if: antagonist potency correlates with aptamer affinity but not with junction bulk.

P3 · dm³ Sigmoid Dose-Response

The dose-response curve (OFF-lock probability vs. antagonist concentration) follows a dm³ sigmoid with Hill coefficient determined by μ_max = −2 and κ*_ribo. This functional form is identical up to morphism scaling to the NGS cluster quality curve (D2) and MT stabiliser response (D3) — the Coherence Bridge prediction. Falsified if: the Hill coefficient deviates from dm³ prediction by > 2σ.

Experimental Protocols

P1 Validation — SHAPE/DMS Probing

Perform SHAPE or DMS probing on the riboswitch at variable Mg²⁺ concentrations. Extract flexibility at the switching junction (nucleotides bracketing the competing stems). Plot inferred free-energy barrier vs. computed κ*_ribo from the dm³ contact structure. Test linearity by regression (R² threshold: 0.9).

P2 Validation — Junction-Bulk Screen

Synthesize a small panel (n ≥ 10) of aminoglycoside analogues varying junction-contact bulk while holding aptamer-core affinity constant. Measure OFF-lock IC₅₀ in a translation reporter assay. If P2 holds: IC₅₀ correlates with junction bulk (ρ > 0.8 Spearman), not Kd to aptamer core.

P3 Validation — Dose-Response Curve Shape

Fit the full OFF-lock probability vs. [antagonist] curve to both a standard Hill equation and the dm³ sigmoid. Test: dm³ fit has lower AIC/BIC. Compare Hill coefficient to dm³ prediction. Additionally: compare the fitted curve (after morphism rescaling) to the NGS cluster quality curve (D2) — if shapes match, the Coherence Bridge Theorem is confirmed across two domains.

Predictions and Solutions for Next-Generation Sequencing Bridge Amplification via Contact-Geometric Operator Analysis. Illumina bridge amplification — the throughput-limiting step in modern short-read sequencing — modeled from first principles using the dm³ operator framework.

↗ Zenodo · D2 / Main Paper v2 · 10.5281/zenodo.20560849 Published · June 2026
Abstract

Library preparation, which governs the quality of material delivered to the flow cell, accounts for a substantial fraction of per-sample NGS cost. Cluster quality depends on two geometric thresholds: a minimum primer density below which bridge formation fails, and a maximum density above which polyclonal co-loading dominates. Both thresholds have not been derived from first principles.

We apply the TOGT/GTCT contact-geometric framework to model bridge amplification as an operator sequence G = U ∘ F ∘ K ∘ C on a dm³ contact manifold. The curvature threshold κ* maps to the critical primer density; the Whitney A₁ fold maps to the cluster branching commitment event.

κ*_NGS ↔ critical primer density
Whitney A₁ fold = cluster branching commitment · First-principles derivation from DNA persistence length
Three Falsifiable Predictions
P1 · First-Principles κ* Prediction

κ*_NGS can be predicted from DNA persistence length and primer spacing without free parameters. Given the persistence length L_p ≈ 50 nm (standard dsDNA) and the flow-cell primer pitch p, the dm³ curvature formula gives κ*_NGS = f(L_p, p) analytically. Falsified if: the observed critical primer density deviates from the prediction by > 15% across > 3 commercial flow-cell geometries.

P2 · Whitney A₁ Bifurcation in Cluster Quality

The cluster quality vs. primer density curve has a Whitney A₁ bifurcation structure near κ*_NGS. This means: (a) the quality–density curve has a cusp, not a smooth sigmoid; (b) hysteresis is observable if density is swept up vs. down; (c) the bifurcation point is sharp (not diffuse) in the thermodynamic limit. Falsified if: quality–density data fit a smooth Hill equation better than the Whitney cusp model (AIC criterion).

P3 · Coherence Bridge — Universality Across Domains

The functional form of the cluster quality vs. primer density curve is identical up to morphism scaling to the riboswitch OFF-lock dose-response (D1) and the MT stabiliser response (D3). After rescaling concentration → κ* and probability → cluster quality score, the three curves should be indistinguishable by KS test. Falsified if: the three rescaled curves differ (KS p < 0.05).

Predictions and Solutions for Microtubule Curvature Modulation: Anesthesia, Neuroprotection, and Tauopathies via Contact-Geometric Operator Analysis. Volatile anesthetics alter collective terahertz oscillations in microtubules in a manner that correlates with clinical potency — a classical (non-quantum) mechanism proposed for the first time with quantitative predictions.

↗ Zenodo · D3 · 10.5281/zenodo.20567325 Published · June 2026
Abstract

Volatile anesthetics (isoflurane, halothane) bind to tubulin and alter collective terahertz oscillations in a manner that correlates with clinical potency [Craddock et al., 2017]. Epothilone B, a brain-penetrant MT stabiliser, delays anesthetic-induced loss of righting reflex in rats with a large effect size (Cohen's d = 1.9) [Khan et al., 2024]. The molecular mechanism linking anesthetic binding, MT curvature, and consciousness remains contested: the Orch-OR theory invokes quantum coherence; no classical mechanism has been proposed with quantitative predictions.

We apply the TOGT/GTCT contact-geometric framework to model MT dynamic instability as an operator sequence G = U ∘ F ∘ K ∘ C on a dm³ contact manifold. The curvature threshold κ*_MT maps to the GTP-tubulin fraction at which catastrophe initiates; the fold operator maps to protofilament peeling commitment.

κ*_MT ↔ GTP-tubulin catastrophe threshold
Fold operator F = protofilament peeling commitment · Anesthetics: Δκ*_MT linear in MAC · Classical, no quantum measurement required
Three Falsifiable Predictions
P1 · Anesthetic Δκ* Linear in MAC

Anesthetic binding produces a measurable downward shift Δκ*_MT linear in MAC (minimum alveolar concentration — the clinical potency unit). At MAC = 1, the dm³ framework predicts a specific fractional shift in the GTP-tubulin fraction at catastrophe onset. Falsified if: the Δκ*_MT vs MAC plot is non-linear (R² < 0.9) across at least 3 volatile agents at 3 MAC values each.

P2 · Epothilone B LORR Delay from dm³ Lipschitz Bound

The epothilone B LORR (loss-of-righting-reflex) delay is proportional to the κ* elevation from the stabiliser dose, with proportionality constant derivable from the dm³ Lipschitz bound — no fitting parameters. Given the measured LORR delay from Khan et al. 2024 (Cohen's d = 1.9), the framework back-calculates a specific κ* elevation that can be independently measured by in vitro MT dynamics assay. Falsified if: the predicted κ* elevation is not reproduced in the in vitro assay within 20%.

P3 · Coherence Bridge — Functional Universality

The dose-response functional form is identical to the riboswitch OFF-lock curve (D1) after morphism rescaling. This is the strongest cross-domain prediction: MT catastrophe probability vs. [anesthetic] rescaled to κ* units should be statistically indistinguishable from the riboswitch OFF-lock probability vs. [antagonist] after the same rescaling. Falsified if: KS test p < 0.05 after optimal morphism rescaling.

Tau as κ* Modulator
Hyperphosphorylated tau reduces MT stabilization by elevating κ* in the wrong direction — making catastrophe more likely under normal conditions. The dm³ framework maps tau phosphorylation state to a specific Δκ* contribution, providing a quantitative disease model for Alzheimer's and related tauopathies.
Therapeutic Implication
Brain-penetrant MT stabilizers (epothilone B, or next-generation derivatives) that restore κ*_MT to the normal range could counteract tau-mediated destabilization. The dm³ Lipschitz bound gives the minimum effective dose from molecular parameters — testable without animal experiments.
