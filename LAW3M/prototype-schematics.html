Device 1 — Three-Disk Plasma Rotor

Three aluminum disks at 0°/120°/240° azimuthal positions, helically stacked along z-axis. Driven by three independent brushless DC motors. Contact manifold: α = dz − r²dθ. Corona ring at r* = 155mm radius. High-voltage option for plasma demonstration.

Device 2 — Jackknife Demo Rig

1:20 scale tractor-trailer. Servo-controlled steering, rotary encoder at hitch joint measuring φ. Arduino Mega controller with real-time Whitney fold detection. LED and buzzer alarm at φ_c = 0.776π. Suitable for conference and classroom demonstration.

Assembly Sequence — Three-Disk Rotor
1
Machine disks D1–D3

Turn each disk to Ø200mm × 6mm on lathe. Cut corona groove at r=155.2mm ± 0.1mm (1.5mm deep × 2mm wide). Drill 6× M5 bolt holes on 170mm PCD. Bore center to 25.015mm H7 for bearing outer race. Face to 0.8 Ra.

2
Press bearings into disks

Press-fit 6305 2RS bearing into each disk center bore. Apply Loctite 641 retaining compound. Allow 24 hr cure before proceeding.

3
Machine base plate BP-01

Machine 400×400×20mm plate. Drill 3× motor mount patterns (4× M6 each, on 280mm PCD at 120°). Bore center 25mm through for shaft. Drill 4× M8 feet. Mill flat within 0.05mm.

4
Install shaft SP-01

Insert shaft through base plate center bore. Secure with M25 locknut from below. Apply keyway at each disk position (z=50, 150, 250mm). Shaft must run true within 0.02mm TIR — check with dial gauge on lathe before assembly.

5
Slide disks onto shaft in azimuth order

D1 at z=50mm, 0° key position. D2 at z=150mm, 120° key position. D3 at z=250mm, 240° key position. Secure each with M25 shaft collar and Loctite 243. Verify 120° azimuthal alignment with a protractor before Loctite cures.

6
Mount motors and belt drives

Bolt 3× BLDC motors to base plate motor mounts. Install pulleys (20T motor, 40T disk) and tension HTD belts to 1% sag measured at mid-span. Secure tensioner with jam nut.

7
Fit corona rings

Wind 2mm bare copper wire to r=155.2mm diameter ring for each disk. Seat in groove; solder ends. Connect HV cable via silicone-insulated wire (rated 40kV) to external HV terminal on base plate. Route cable in channel, avoid sharp bends.

8
Wire electronics and test

Connect ESCs to motors. Connect Arduino to ESCs (PWM signal) and RPM sensors (Hall effect). Upload sync_motors.ino sketch (ramp to 300 rpm, verify all three reach synchronised θ̇ = 1 within 2%). Log data to serial.

9
Safety cage and HV commissioning

Assemble acrylic cage around rotor. Wire cage door microswitch as HV interlock — HV supply cannot energise unless cage is closed. Connect HV supply output to external terminal. Test interlock before first HV energisation. Start at 1kV, observe corona ring, increment to threshold. Do not exceed 30kV.

SAFETY: High-voltage operation requires a second person present at all times. All operators must wear rubber-soled shoes. Never open cage with HV enabled. Emergency stop button must be within arm's reach. Earth the base plate.

Assembly Sequence — Jackknife Demo Rig
1
3D print all PLA parts

Print T-01 (tractor), T-02 (trailer), T-03 (front axle), and wheel sets at 0.2mm layer height, 30% gyroid infill. Support material on tractor cab and trailer kingpin plate. Estimated print time: 14 hours total.

2
Assemble front axle and steering

Press M3 threaded inserts (heat-set) into servo mount points on tractor body. Mount MG996R servo in tractor cab. Connect servo horn to front axle tie rod (M3 threaded rod, 2× M3 ball links). Test steering: servo at centre = wheels straight; ±35° servo travel = ±35° wheel turn.

3
Install hitch joint and encoder

Stack trailer kingpin plate over tractor fifth-wheel plate. Insert M6×30mm bolt as pivot. Add nylon washer above and below. Press diametric magnet into centre of bolt head (epoxy). Mount AS5600 board on trailer kingpin plate face, 1.5–3mm gap to magnet. Centre magnet over encoder IC using a non-ferrous spacer jig. Secure with M3 screws. Verify I2C reads 0x36.

4
Wire electronics on stripboard

Lay out Arduino Mega, BEC, LDO, encoder header, OLED header, LED resistors, and buzzer on 100×80mm stripboard per Drawing J-02. Solder all connections. Add 100µF caps across 5V and 3.3V rails. Run I2C bus with 4.7kΩ pullups to 3.3V. Mount board in tractor body using M3 standoffs.

5
Upload Arduino sketch

Connect via USB. Open jackknife_fold.ino in Arduino IDE. Set TRAILER_WHEELBASE_MM = 270.0 and SERVO_MAX_ANGLE = 35.0. Upload. Open serial monitor at 115200 baud. Verify: φ reads in degrees, φ_c displays correctly, bar graph on OLED responds to manual hitch movement.

6
Calibrate and test alarm thresholds

With rig on flat surface, push trailer manually to various angles. Verify yellow LED at φ = 0.9 × φ_c (≈ 126°), red LED + buzzer at 0.95 × φ_c (≈ 133°). Verify OLED shows logarithmic escape time Tₑ ~ −log|φ − φ_c| / g'(φ_c) as φ approaches φ_c. This is the Whitney fold signature.

7
Label and mount to display base

Mount finished rig on 300×200mm black acrylic base. Attach printed label: "Jackknife = Whitney A₁ Fold · Contact Manifold (ℝ, dφ − dθ + sinφ/L · ds) · doi:10.5281/zenodo.19117399". Mount OLED on angled bracket for viewing at poster eye level.

Arduino sketch (jackknife_fold.ino) computes in real time: κ₀ from servo PWM width → φ_c = π − arcsin(L × κ₀) → Tₑ = −1/g'(φ_c) × log|φ − φ_c| → display all three on OLED. Source committed to AXLE/LAW3M/firmware/.
