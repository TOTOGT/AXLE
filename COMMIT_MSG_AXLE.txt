research/dps/  Duplicate Provisioning Survey (DPS-1), pre-registration draft.
  Measures the number WP-36 needs. Behaviour asked before attribution;
  competing explanations offered as equals; a revealed-preference tradeoff that
  is costly to answer strategically. Reporting rule fixed in advance: publish
  both estimates, lead with the lower. power.py: n=29 for "more than a
  quarter"; n=48 to show the tradeoff is not a coin flip; n~150 suffices.
  Frame bias, not n, is the binding constraint.
