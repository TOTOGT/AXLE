The Framework

2.1 The Operator Chain

TOGT formalises how dynamic systems navigate possibility space along topologically protected paths using a recursive, non-commutative operator chain. Six operators compose as:

G = U ■ F ■ K ■ C  (+T for generative cycling)

The composition is non-commutative: order matters. Each operator acts on the system's contact-geometry manifold.

2.2 dm³ Contact Manifolds

A dm³ manifold (three-dimensional contact manifold) is the geometric substrate on which the operator chain acts. In biological systems these manifolds appear as energy landscapes with basins, ridges, saddle points, and curvature thresholds (κ*). Small perturbations — ligand binding, drug action, mechanical force — shift κ* values, redirecting the system from one generative path to another.

2.3 The Coherence Bridge Theorem

Any two systems sharing the same operator chain, curvature threshold κ*, frequency ω, and maximum Lyapunov exponent µ_max on dm³ manifolds are related by explicit contact morphisms — they are categorically equivalent. This is not analogy: it is a formal proof that geometric invariants transfer exactly between domains.

§ 3 — Research Projects

Three High-Impact
Translational Domains

01
Genomics
NGS Library Preparation Optimization
$50M–$200M+ Annual savings
+

Bridge amplification on flow cells is inherently a surface topographic process — DNA strands undergo constrained diffusion, folding, and geometric clustering under strict curvature and contact constraints. TOGT provides the natural theoretical scaffold to model this as an operator-driven transition on a dm³ manifold, moving beyond empirical optimisation to predictive, geometry-first design.

This proposal is a direct theoretical extension of the 2026 Princess of Asturias Award-winning bridge amplification technology (Klenerman, Balasubramanian, Mayer).

Specific Aims

Aim 1Develop a TOGT/GTCT computational model of bridge amplification. Map C (surface attachment), K (primer density/curvature constraints κ*), F (cluster growth branching), U (viable cluster selection), and T (saturation circuit) on the flow-cell contact manifold. Extend Lean 4 proofs to this domain.
Aim 2Identify and validate topographic interventions. Predict surface modifications, primer density patterns, or buffer additives that lower amplification bias. Synthesise and test 3–5 TOGT-guided surface variants.
Aim 3Demonstrate simplified library preparation protocols. Benchmark against standard kits on Illumina/NovaSeq-class platforms for yield, uniformity, GC bias, and sequencing quality.

Expected: 40–60% reduction in library preparation costs. $20–$150 savings per sample.

02
Infectious Disease
Aminoglycoside Resistance Riboswitch Antagonists
$1B–$5B Annual savings
+

Antibiotic resistance costs an estimated $66 billion per year globally, projected to reach $159 billion annually by 2050. Many clinical isolates carry aminoglycoside-sensing riboswitches that induce resistance enzyme expression only when drug is present. Synthetic antagonists that lock the Shine-Dalgarno sequence in its sequestered default would block inducible resistance and restore sensitivity.

Lead Candidate: Neo-Antag-1 — a steric-bulky neamine derivative. The neamine core provides polycationic pocket affinity while an N-phenyl substitution introduces steric hindrance against the switching junction.

Additional Candidates

PolyCatAntag-2: Bis-guanidinium/benzimidazole scaffold that locks the apo OFF conformation by bridging phosphates without triggering stem rearrangement.
PNAAntag-3: Short peptide nucleic acid complementary to the switching sequence plus a polycationic head — sterically blocks anti-SD2/SD1 pairing.

First TOGT-derived small-molecule antagonists for an antibiotic resistance riboswitch. Proof-of-concept for a new class of 'resistance blockers'.

03
Neuropharmacology
Microtubule Curvature Modulation for Anesthesia & Neuroprotection
$500M–$2B+ Annual savings
+

Volatile anesthetics (isoflurane, halothane) bind tubulin, modulate critical curvature thresholds (κ*), and dampen collective terahertz oscillations — effects that correlate with loss and recovery of consciousness. TOGT provides the first rigorous, operator-based geometric model of these dynamics.

The Coherence Bridge directly transfers validated insights from riboswitches and NGS surface chemistry into this domain, enabling predictive design of selective κ* modulators for safer anesthesia, faster emergence, reduced postoperative delirium, and neuroprotection in tauopathies and TBI.

Specific Aims

Aim 1Extend Lean 4 proofs to anesthetic-induced κ* shifts on cylindrical dm³ manifolds; simulate catastrophe/rescue transitions and collective vibrational modes.
Aim 2In silico screening of κ* modulators — design small-molecule candidates that selectively counter anesthetic-induced κ* changes without full sedation reversal.
Aim 3In vitro tubulin polymerisation assays, single-molecule curvature measurements, THz spectroscopy; rodent anesthesia emergence and neuroprotection readouts.

Neuroprotective pipeline for tauopathies, Alzheimer's disease, and traumatic brain injury.
