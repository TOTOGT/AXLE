Research that states how it knows.

G6 is an independent research company. It prepares federal research proposals, builds quantitative analyses that someone else can re-run, and formalises mathematical results in a proof assistant.

Every claim carries its evidentiary status. A figure is labelled by how it was established before anyone relies on it.

Services

Federal proposal preparation and compliance

Scientific and technical narratives, budgets, data management plans, and the compliance work that decides whether a proposal is reviewed at all: page and typography limits measured from the rendered file, dual-anonymous redaction verified by scan rather than by assertion, generative-AI disclosure, and reconciliation of every submitted figure against the form it was entered on.

For small businesses and independent investigators pursuing NASA, NIH and NSF awards.

Auditable quantitative analysis

Analyses in Python against public scientific archives, written so that each number traces to the file and the line of code that produced it. Results are validated against independently computed bounds instead of a single run, and corrections are logged with their dates rather than quietly absorbed.

For organisations whose numbers will be contested, audited, or cited.

Formal verification in Lean 4

Mathematical results stated as machine-checkable theorems, with the unproved assumptions enumerated rather than left implicit. A proof either compiles or it does not, which makes the result checkable by a third party who trusts neither the author nor the prose.

For work where a derivation has to be defensible to someone adversarial.

Method

MEASURED

Computed here, with the script and input named.

READ

Quoted from a source that was opened, with the citation.

ASSUMED

A choice, stated as a choice, with its reason.

UNKNOWN

Not established. Left unknown rather than filled in.

These four labels are attached to every quantity in a G6 deliverable. A number whose basis cannot be confirmed is reported as unconfirmed, including when the document is already signed and the convenient thing would be silence.
