Funder-Fit Map · The Reintegration Engine (NJ–NY)

Which door funds which half — and how to frame it

G6 LLC · Pablo Nogueira Grossi · Newark, NJ · Draft 2026 · research service both

No single funder pays for the whole vision, because agencies are themselves siloed — the thing we are trying to heal. So we aim each half at the door built for it, and reserve the one vehicle (NSF's use-inspired side) that is scored on both. Below: what each funder will pay for, what it won't, and the exact frame that fits its mandate.

Federal — the large vehicles

FunderAxisFundsWon't fundFrame it as
NSF — TIP / Regional Innovation Engines both Use-inspired research + workforce + regional economic benefit, as one coalition award ($15M/2yr → up to $160M/decade). A solo LLC as lead; pure charity with no research or tech thrust; anything without a regional coalition and matching funds. A NYC–NJ innovation cluster for human capital and learning technology — the corridor with no Engine. Requires a university/nonprofit lead.
NSF — EDU / DRL research Research on STEM teaching and learning, formal and informal settings. ESL / language service; program delivery; anything not framed as STEM-learning research. A study of how immigrant adults learn mathematics up a formality gradient — the math, not the English, is the way in.
NSF — SBE (behavioral/cognitive) research Cognition, learning science, the mechanism behind the cognitive-reserve claim. Service delivery; clinical/aging trials (that's NIH's lane). The basic-science question: does the formal-language dose add to bilingual cognitive reserve?
NIH / NIA (National Institute on Aging) research Cognitive aging, dementia delay, the "brain-years-younger" endpoint as a health outcome. Curriculum development for its own sake; non-health education. A multilingual-learning intervention measured for delayed cognitive aging — the 12-years-younger hypothesis, honestly bounded.
Dept. of Education — OCTAE (WIOA Title II) / IES service Adult basic education, adult ESL, family literacy (OCTAE); education research (IES). Cognitive-neuroscience aims; anything not tied to adult learner outcomes. Bilingual adult numeracy + literacy for immigrant families — the direct-service half.
NEH (National Endowment for the Humanities) both Language, translation, the humanities side of a reintegrated arts-and-sciences program. The STEM/lab science; direct social service. Comparative language and translation as public humanities — the 40-language LaTeX/babel scaling as cultural infrastructure.
SBIR / STTR (NSF, incl. TIP) both Small-business R&D; STTR explicitly pairs a company with a research institution. Non-commercial pure service; projects with no path to a product/platform. The learning platform (open curriculum + the auto-translating LaTeX engine) as a fundable small-business technology — the one place G6 LLC can be the lead.

Regional & private — the service half

FunderAxisFundsWon't fundFrame it as
NJ State Council on the Arts · Newark Arts · NYSCA service Arts programming and arts-in-education; the reintegration's arts half. Lab science; cognitive research. Arts-and-sciences reintegration as community arts practice.
Immigrant / community foundations (e.g., NJ + NYC funders, Dodge) service Direct service to immigrant families and people with disabilities; pilots. Basic research; capital-heavy asks. A free, bilingual, disability-inclusive education gift — the Newark/NYC pilot.
Archdiocese / parish networks + city micro-grants service Seed amounts ($100-scale church support; NYC program funds) to stand up the first classroom. Anything requiring formal research infrastructure. The gift program — the $100-from-the-church pilot that becomes a template for parishes worldwide.
Corporate / tech philanthropy (learning + AI) both Open educational tech, AI-for-learning, multilingual access at scale. Undifferentiated charity with no platform story. Open, auto-translating curriculum reaching 40 languages — access infrastructure.
The through-line. Every federal research door wants the cognitive-reserve study (Intellectual Merit); every service door wants the free bilingual classroom (Broader Impacts). NSF's Engines / TIP and STTR are the only vehicles that pay for both at once — and of those, STTR is the single door where G6 LLC can be the applicant of record (a company paired with a research institution). Everywhere else, the sequence is: recruit the academic anchor first, then apply through it.
